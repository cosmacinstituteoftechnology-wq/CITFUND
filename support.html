Frequently Asked

1
How do I deposit money?

Tap the ⊕ button at the bottom of your dashboard, choose your network (MTN, Telecel, or AirtelTigo), enter the amount and your phone number, then approve the prompt on your phone. Funds appear in your wallet automatically.

2
What are the transaction fees?

Fees are tiered based on the amount:
• GHS 1–50: GHS 1.00 flat
• GHS 100–199: 1.5%
• GHS 200–300: 2.7%
• Higher amounts: as low as 1.0%

Internal transfers between CITFUND members are FREE.

3
How long does withdrawal take?

Withdrawals via Paystack transfer usually complete in seconds to a few minutes. If there's a delay, the amount is automatically refunded to your CITFUND wallet within 24 hours.

4
How do I earn 5% every 15 days?

Invest any amount from GHS 10 (Flex plans) or GHS 300 (Fixed plans) in the Investments page. Your money earns 5% every 15 days automatically. Plans run from 15 days up to 5 years.

5
I forgot my PIN — what do I do?

Go to Profile → Security → Reset PIN. You'll receive a One-Time Password (OTP) on your registered phone. Enter it and set a new PIN. If you can't receive the OTP, contact support immediately.

6
Why is my KYC taking long?

KYC submissions are typically reviewed within 24 hours. Delays usually happen when: documents are blurry, names don't match, or the selfie isn't clear. You'll get an SMS with a decision.

7
How do I apply for a loan?

Loans unlock after 120 days of active account use. Then go to the Loans page, choose a product (Instant, Business, Personal or Emergency), enter the amount, and submit. Approval takes up to 24 hours.

8
Can I send money to a friend?

Yes! Transfers between CITFUND members are instant and FREE. On the dashboard tap Transfer, enter your friend's CITFUND account number and the amount — done. Both parties receive SMS confirmation.
